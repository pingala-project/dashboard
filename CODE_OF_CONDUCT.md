# Pingala Code of Conduct

Pingala is a learning project. Participate with patience, curiosity, and respect for people at different levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized behavior toward minors, deliberate misinformation, spam, and hostile personal attacks.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and useful. Report safety or privacy concerns privately through `SECURITY.md` rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
